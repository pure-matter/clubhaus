Why Clubhaus

Somewhere to study, build and meet. Close to home.

Community spaces are scarce in The Gambia, and the few that exist are rarely free, reliable or close to where people live. Young people who want to build skills in technology, security, STEM or development have nowhere dependable to study, work or meet, and few ways to hear about the grants, jobs and programmes open to them.

Clubhaus is a nonprofit community library and learning hub. We're proving the model in one community, then we'll document it and help others run their own.

Learn

Free and low-cost workshops in technology, security, STEM and development, starting with practical personal security. Founder spotlights, and a regular digest of grants, free tools, jobs and programmes.

Read

A community library growing to 1,000 titles: education and skills, African literature and history, children and youth, and reference and technology.

Work + meet

Bookable meeting rooms, workstations, study cubes and a lab, with reliable internet, backup power and printing.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to build skills.

Students Job seekers & recent graduates Freelancers & creatives Early-stage founders NGOs & community groups

Mostly 18 to 35. Free for students 25 and under. GMD 50 a month for adults once we open.

Ways to help

Give what you have.

Books

Donate books, run a book drive, or fund a shelf. We're collecting across four collections.

Money

A one-off gift or a small monthly one. Diaspora support goes a long way here.

Time & skills

Run a workshop, help in the library, host an event, or help with legal work, renovation or finance.
