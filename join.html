The fine print, in plain English

Agreements.

Membership agreement

Draft · To be reviewed by a lawyer before Clubhaus is registered

By becoming a member, you agree to the following.

1. Membership

  • Anyone can be a member. Membership is free for students 25 and under, and GMD 50 a month for adults once the space opens.
  • Before we open, members join the founding members list. There's nothing to pay.
  • Your membership is personal. Please don't lend your card or account to anyone else.

2. Borrowing books

  • Look after what you borrow and return it on time.
  • If a book is lost or damaged, tell us. We'll work out a fair way to replace it together.

3. Using the space

  • Book rooms, desks and study cubes in advance, and cancel if you can't make it.
  • Keep study areas quiet. Leave each space as you found it.
  • Take care of the computers, printers and furniture, and report anything broken.

4. How we treat each other

  • Be respectful. No harassment, discrimination, threats or bullying.
  • Clubhaus welcomes young people. Everyone helps keep it safe for them.
  • Don't use our internet or devices for anything illegal or harmful.

5. Your information

  • We collect only what we need to run your membership, such as your name, contact details and age group.
  • We never sell it or share it outside Clubhaus. Ask us at any time to see or delete it.

6. Leaving or being asked to leave

  • You can end your membership at any time by telling us.
  • If someone breaks this agreement, we may pause or end their membership. We'll explain why and listen first.
Contributor agreement

Draft · To be reviewed by a lawyer before Clubhaus is registered

This agreement is for people and organisations who help build Clubhaus but are not staff or board members.

1. What a contributor is

  • You give time, skills, books, equipment or money to help Clubhaus happen.
  • Contributing is voluntary and unpaid unless we agree otherwise in writing. Expenses are paid back only if agreed beforehand.

2. Your commitment

  • Do what you said you'd do, and tell us early if your plans change. Stepping back is always fine.

3. What "owning a part" means

  • Claiming a part of the Haus, like a shelf, collection or workshop series, means you steward it and get credit for it.
  • It's not a legal or financial stake. Clubhaus is a nonprofit, and any surplus goes back into the library and programmes.

4. Gifts of money and things

  • We use your gift for the part you chose. If that part is already fully funded, or plans change, we'll use it for the closest need and tell you.
  • Donated items become Clubhaus property.

5. Acting for Clubhaus

  • Don't sign agreements, spend money, collect donations or speak to the media for Clubhaus without written approval from the founder.

6. Work you create

  • You keep credit for workshop materials, designs and writing you make for Clubhaus.
  • You give Clubhaus permission to use, share and adapt them, usually under a Creative Commons Attribution licence (CC BY 4.0), unless we agree otherwise.

7. Conduct and safeguarding

  • Follow the same code of conduct as members.
  • When working with anyone under 18, never be alone with them out of sight of others. Report any concern to the founder straight away.

8. Privacy and conflicts of interest

  • Keep members' personal information private.
  • Tell us if you, or someone close to you, could benefit from a Clubhaus decision you're involved in.

9. Recognition

  • Contributors are thanked as Friends of the Haus on the website and in the space, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

10. Stepping back

  • You can stop contributing at any time. Please return any keys, equipment or files that belong to Clubhaus.
